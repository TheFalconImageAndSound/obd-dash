OBD Dash

by Falcon Image and Sound

Read & clear check-engine codes and watch live engine data with your Veepeak OBDCheck BLE+.

  1. Plug the adapter into the OBD port under the dashboard (driver’s side).
  2. Turn the ignition ON or start the engine.
  3. Tap Connect and choose VEEPEAK from the list. Don’t pair it in your phone’s Bluetooth settings first.

          Clear trouble codes?

          This erases stored and pending codes and turns off the check-engine light.

          • Write the codes down first (or use “Copy report”). The freeze frame is erased too.
          • Readiness monitors reset. The car will fail an emissions/smog test until you’ve driven enough for them to finish — often 50–100+ miles over several days.
          • For best results: engine off, ignition on. Some cars refuse while running.
          • If the problem isn’t fixed, the light will come back.

          Dashboard gauges

          RPM and speed are always shown. Pick the tiles you want; fewer tiles means faster updates.
